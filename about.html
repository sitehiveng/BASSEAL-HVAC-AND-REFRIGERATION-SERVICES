About AC Tech

A Lagos-based HVAC engineering team built on precise workmanship, honest pricing, and a genuine 24-hour commitment to the people who rely on us.

COMPANY OVERVIEW
// COMPANY OVERVIEW

Built on-site, not in a boardroom

AC Tech by Basseal HVAC — the trading name of Basseal HVAC and Refrigeration Services Ltd — started with a simple frustration: too many HVAC jobs in Lagos were rushed, under-specified, or abandoned after payment. We built our practice around the opposite — technicians who size systems correctly, document their work, and stand behind it long after the invoice is settled. Today we serve homeowners, businesses, and religious institutions across the city, from single-room split units to full commercial VRF installations.

// WHAT MAKES US DIFFERENT

Engineering discipline, not guesswork

DIFF / 01

Load-calculated installs

Every install starts with a proper room and building load calculation — not a rule-of-thumb unit size. That's the difference between a system that keeps up on the hottest day and one that runs constantly and still underperforms.

DIFF / 02

Transparent, itemized quotes

You see labor, parts, and refrigerant costs broken down before work begins. No call-out surprises, no inflated "diagnostic fees" added after the fact.

DIFF / 03

Technicians who document

Every job leaves you with a written record of what was installed or repaired, the parts used, and the recommended service interval — useful for warranties and resale alike.

DIFF / 04

Real 24-hour coverage

Our emergency line is answered by a technician, not a call center reading a script. If it's urgent, we treat it that way.

// OUR APPROACH TO EVERY PROJECT

From first call to final walkthrough

STEP / ASSESS

Site assessment & load calculation

We inspect the space, measure heat load factors, and confirm electrical and structural requirements before recommending equipment.

STEP / QUOTE

Itemized proposal

You receive a clear written quote covering equipment, labor, materials, and estimated timeline — no verbal-only estimates.

STEP / EXECUTE

Installation or repair

Work is carried out by trained technicians following manufacturer specification and local code, with daily progress updates on multi-day jobs.

STEP / TEST

Commissioning & testing

Every system is pressure-tested, charged to spec, and run through a full cooling cycle before we consider the job complete.

STEP / HANDOVER

Walkthrough & documentation

We walk you through the system, hand over documentation, and schedule your first preventive maintenance visit.

// SAFETY & QUALITY COMMITMENT

Standards we don't compromise on

Certified refrigerant handling

Technicians follow proper recovery, evacuation and charging procedures to prevent leaks and environmental harm.

Electrical safety first

Every install includes correct breaker sizing, earthing, and isolator installation to protect your property.

Workmanship guarantee

Installations and repairs are backed by a written guarantee, with follow-up inspections included.
